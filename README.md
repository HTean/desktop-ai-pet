# Desktop AI Pet

Desktop AI Pet is a small companion that lives directly on your desktop.

It combines a virtual pet with task management and reminders, so your pet can help keep track of what you need to do.

## ✨ Main Features

* 🐶 **Desktop Pet** — drag, interact with, and talk to your pet through speech bubbles
* ✅ **Task Management** — add, edit, complete, delete, and repeat tasks
* ⏰ **Pet Reminders** — your pet pops up when something needs your attention
* 🔁 **Recurring Tasks** — daily, weekly, and monthly tasks with recurring reminders
* 🌐 **English & 中文** — switch languages from the system tray
* 💾 **Local Storage** — tasks and reminders stay on your computer

## 💬 Talk to Nainiu

Double-click Nainiu to open the chat box.

### Commands

`add task Buy groceries`
`添加任务 买菜`

Adds a task without a scheduled time.

`show my tasks`
`查看任务`

Shows how many unfinished tasks you currently have.

`open tasks`
`打开任务`

Opens the full Tasks window.

### Natural Language Tasks

For tasks with a date or time, you can simply describe them:

```text
Meeting tomorrow at 3 PM
明天下午3点开会

Every Monday at 9 AM team meeting
每周一上午9点开会
```

Nainiu will recognize the task and time and ask you to confirm it before saving.

## ⏰ Reminders

When creating or editing a task, you can choose:

* At task time
* 5 / 15 / 30 minutes before
* 1 hour before
* Custom reminder time

When the reminder is due, Nainiu will pop up with:

**Complete** — finish the task
**Later** — remind you again in 5, 15, 30, or 60 minutes

If the app was closed when a reminder became due, the missed reminder will appear the next time the app starts.

## 🌐 Language

Right-click the system tray icon:

```text
Language
├─ English
└─ 中文
```

Your language selection is saved and will remain active after restarting the app.

## Download & Install

### Windows

1. Go to the **Releases** section of this GitHub repository.
2. Open the latest release.
3. Download:

```text
AI Desktop Pet Setup.exe
```

4. Double-click the installer.
5. Follow the installation steps.
6. Launch **AI Desktop Pet**.

After launching, Nainiu will appear on your desktop.

You can also find the application in the Windows system tray, where you can:

* Show the pet
* Hide the pet
* Quit the application

No Internet connection is required for the basic desktop pet functionality.
